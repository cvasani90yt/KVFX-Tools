Motion Lab

Ten interactions to choose from for the KVFX site. Each one is live: move, click, drag and scroll to try it. Pick the numbers you like and they can go on the site.

Already on the site: the hero grid behind “Less busywork. More motion.” Hover to light the dots, click for a ripple, and drag to draw a selection box that picks up the words it touches.

01

Stagger Grid

Click any square and a wave runs out from it, one square after another. The orders are the ones Sequence Layers offers, so the demo shows the feature as well as decorating the page.

Fits

Sequence Layers in the top ten, or the background of the pricing card.

02

Magnetic Buttons

Move toward a button and it leans to meet the cursor, its label a little further. Move away and it springs back with a small wobble.

Fits

The hero's buy button, the pricing button and the play buttons.

Needs a mouse or trackpad.

pull radius 120 px
03

Text Presets

A headline that animates in by word or by letter, like the panel's Text Animate presets. Lift on hover raises the letters under the cursor.

Fits

Every section heading as it scrolls into view, and the hero headline on load.

Make every word land.

04

Live Curve

Drag the two handles and the layer moves with that ease straight away. The dots under it are its frames: bunched where it is slow, spread out where it is fast. Pull a handle past the box for overshoot.

Fits

The Curve Editor slot in the top ten, next to its clip.

cubic-bezier(0.80, 0.00, 0.20, 1.00)
05

Tilt Cards

Cards turn toward the cursor in 3D, and a light follows it across the card and its edge. The icon and title float above the card, so the depth shows as it turns.

Fits

The “Inside the panel” cards for the eight tabs.

Needs a mouse or trackpad.

Comp Resizer

16:9 to vertical in one click.

Cut the pauses from a voiceover.

Follow Layer

Follow without parenting.

06

Scroll Timeline

Scrolling the page plays a comp. The playhead, the timecode and the keyframes all follow the scroll, and run backwards when you scroll back up. You can also drag along the timeline to scrub.

Fits

A “How it works” section that walks through one command as visitors scroll.

07

Velocity Marquee

Two bands of command names drift past. Scroll and they speed up and lean with the scroll; scroll up and they turn around. Grab a band and throw it.
