Questions

Good to know

Which versions of After Effects does it work with?

After Effects 2022 (version 22) and later, on Windows and macOS. Replace Fonts needs After Effects 24.5 or later.

How do I install it?

Unzip the download and run the installer for your system. Restart After Effects, then open Window ▸ Extensions ▸ KVFX Tools.

Will it change my project without asking?

No. Commands only act on the layers you have selected, and the ones that change a lot at once, like resizing comps, cutting pauses or relinking footage, show you what will happen and wait for you to confirm.

Can I undo a command?

Yes. Every command is a single step in Edit ▸ Undo, however many changes it made.

Does it work in other languages of After Effects?

Yes. Commands work in every language of After Effects. The panel itself is in English, and Split uses After Effects' English menu name.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There's nothing to sign in to or activate. Install it and it works offline.
